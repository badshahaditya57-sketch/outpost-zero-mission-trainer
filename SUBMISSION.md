## Project name
Outpost Zero - Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

## Summary (about 100 words)
Outpost Zero is a browser game that teaches students how engineers balance life support, power, food and radiation shielding on the Moon and Mars. Players get a limited build budget, design an outpost on a grid, predict what will fail first, then run a 20-sol stress test with lunar nights, dust storms and solar flares. A chart and plain-language debrief show what happened and why. Difficulty levels, shareable challenge codes and a printable certificate make it easy to use in a classroom. It runs in any browser, with no install or account.

## Project details (starting points)
- **What it does:** design, predict, test, learn, redesign.
- **Benefit/impact:** makes engineering trade-offs tangible and fun for young learners; works on school computers and phones.
- **Tools:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript; hosted on Render.
- **Creative parts:** design-first loop, prediction step, seeded "challenge codes" so every classmate faces the same events.
- **Factors considered:** life support, power storage, food, radiation, accessibility (keyboard, readable contrast, reduced motion).
